3. OBJECTIVE OF THE CONTRACT

The objective of this Annual Maintenance Contract is to ensure the proper working condition, safety, reliability and continuous availability of all vehicles, machines and equipment listed in this document. The service provider shall provide preventive maintenance, breakdown repairs, inspection, servicing, troubleshooting and technical support during the contract period.
